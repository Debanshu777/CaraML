# License and commercial use

CaraML's first-party code is offered under **PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0**. The [LICENSE file](https://github.com/Debanshu777/CaraML/blob/main/LICENSE) contains the governing text. This page is a practical summary, not a replacement for it.

## Noncommercial use

The license permits noncommercial use, modification, and distribution subject to its conditions. Personal study, hobby projects, and qualifying research/testing without anticipated commercial application are examples. It also expressly permits use by the categories of noncommercial organizations listed in the license.

Genuinely noncommercial evaluation is permitted. Calling a business pilot an “evaluation,” running it internally, or offering it for free does not by itself make the purpose noncommercial. If an evaluation supports an anticipated commercial application, request permission before relying on it.

Keep the license and required notices with copies, including modified versions. The license does not authorize selling CaraML or using its covered code for commercial purposes.

## Commercial use requires written permission

For commercial use, including commercial internal deployment, incorporating CaraML code into a paid product, commercial redistribution, or a commercial hosted service, request a separate written agreement from the relevant rights holder before proceeding.

[Request commercial permission](https://github.com/Debanshu777/CaraML/issues/new?template=commercial_permission.yml){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

The request form is public. Share only your organization/project, a non-confidential use summary, and the components/version you want to use. Arrange a suitable private channel for sensitive details. Opening an issue, receiving an acknowledgement, or receiving no reply does **not** grant permission.

Commercial permission can cover only rights the licensor holds or is authorized to grant. Third-party contributions may require additional permission; no contributor copyright assignment is implied by a pull request.

## Why this license?

The project intends to allow noncommercial learning, experimentation, and contributions while reserving commercial use for explicit agreements.

- MIT and Apache-2.0 permit commercial use and resale.
- GPL/AGPL also permit commercial use; their sharing obligations are not a commercial-permission requirement.
- Commons Clause restricts certain sales, but does not require permission for every commercial internal use.
- FSL converts to MIT or Apache-2.0 after two years, which does not fit a lasting commercial-use restriction.

PolyForm Noncommercial is a closer standard fit for this policy. It is **source-available, not OSI-approved open source**, because it restricts commercial use. Organizations should have counsel review their particular use and any separate commercial agreement.
